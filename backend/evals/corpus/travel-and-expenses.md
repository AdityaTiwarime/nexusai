# Travel and Expense Policy

## Booking travel
Train travel is preferred for trips under four hours. Flights must be booked in economy class.
All travel must be booked through the company travel portal at least 7 days in advance.

## Expenses
Submit expense claims within 30 days, with receipts attached, through the expense tool.
The daily meal allowance on business trips is 1,500 rupees.
Hotel stays are reimbursed up to 6,000 rupees per night in metro cities.
Expenses above 25,000 rupees need approval from a department head.
