# Globex Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can plan your own schedule.
Employees may work from home up to two days per week with their manager's approval.

## Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid leave per year.
Unused leave of up to 5 days can be carried over to the next calendar year.
Sick leave does not count against paid leave; a doctor's note is needed after 3 consecutive sick days.
Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Probation
New joiners have a probation period of 6 months. During probation the notice period is 15 days.
After probation the notice period is 60 days.
