# IT Security Policy

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords are rotated every 180 days.
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and the HR portal.

## Devices
Company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 2 hours.
Personal USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.

## Phishing
Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button in Outlook.
The security team reviews reported emails within one business day.
