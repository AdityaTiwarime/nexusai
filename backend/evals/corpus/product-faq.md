# Globex Cloud Analytics: Product FAQ

## Plans
The Starter plan supports up to 5 users and 10 GB of data. The Business plan supports up to 50 users and 500 GB.
Enterprise customers get unlimited users, single sign-on and a dedicated support manager.

## Data retention
Raw event data is kept for 13 months. Aggregated reports are kept indefinitely.
Customers can export all their data as CSV or Parquet at any time.

## Support
Support is available 24/7 for Enterprise customers. Business plan customers get support on weekdays from 9:00 to 18:00 IST.
The target first response time for critical incidents is 30 minutes.
