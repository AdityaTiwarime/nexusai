# Engineering Onboarding Guide

## First week
On day one you receive your laptop, badge and accounts. Your buddy will walk you through the codebase.
Complete the security awareness training within your first 5 working days.

## Development process
All code changes go through pull requests and need at least one approving review.
The main branch is deployed to production every weekday at 14:00 after the test suite passes.
Hotfixes can be deployed outside the schedule with approval from the on-call engineer.

## On-call
Engineers join the on-call rotation after 3 months. A rotation lasts one week.
On-call engineers must acknowledge pages within 15 minutes.
